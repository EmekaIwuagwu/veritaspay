## Getting Started

### Step 1: Get a Wallet (5 minutes)

A wallet is like a digital bank account that holds your VPUSD.

**Best Wallets for Beginners**:

#### **MetaMask** (Most Popular)
1. Go to https://metamask.io
2. Click "Download"
3. Install browser extension (Chrome, Firefox, Brave)
4. Click "Create a new wallet"
5. Create a strong password
6. **IMPORTANT**: Write down your 12-word recovery phrase
7. Done! Your wallet is ready

#### **Trust Wallet** (Mobile)
1. Download from App Store / Google Play
2. Open app → "Create a new wallet"
3. Write down recovery phrase
4. Set up PIN
5. Done!

### Step 2: Secure Your Recovery Phrase

Your **recovery phrase** (12 words) is THE MOST IMPORTANT THING.

**If you lose it = you lose your money forever. No recovery possible.**

✅ **DO**:
- Write it on paper
- Store in a safe place
- Make 2-3 copies
- Keep in different locations
- Never take photos of it

❌ **DON'T**:
- Save on computer
- Email to yourself
- Store in cloud
- Take screenshots
- Share with anyone (even "support")

### Step 3: Add Polygon Network

VPUSD uses Polygon (cheaper fees than Ethereum).

**In MetaMask**:
1. Click network dropdown (top)
2. Click "Add Network"
3. Click "Add a network manually"
4. Enter:
   ```
   Network Name: Polygon
   RPC URL: https://polygon-rpc.com
   Chain ID: 137
   Currency Symbol: MATIC
   Block Explorer: https://polygonscan.com
   ```
5. Click "Save"
6. Switch to Polygon network

**In Trust Wallet**:
1. Polygon is already added!
2. Just search for "VPUSD" to add the token

### Step 4: Get Your Address

This is like your bank account number.

**MetaMask**:
- Click account name at top
- Click the address to copy
- Example: `0x742d35Cc6634C0532925a3b844Bc9e7595f0bEb`

**Trust Wallet**:
- Tap "Receive"
- Select "Polygon"
- Copy address or show QR code

## Security Tips

### Protect Your Wallet

✅ **DO**:
- Use strong, unique password
- Enable 2FA where available
- Keep recovery phrase offline
- Use hardware wallet for large amounts
- Verify addresses before sending
- Start with small test transaction
- Keep software updated

❌ **DON'T**:
- Share recovery phrase with anyone
- Click suspicious links
- Download unknown apps
- Connect to unsecure WiFi
- Keep large amounts in hot wallet
- Take screenshots of sensitive info

### Common Scams to Avoid

**1. Fake Support**
- Real support NEVER asks for recovery phrase
- Never DM first
- Don't click links in DMs

**2. Phishing Websites**
- Always check URL carefully
- Use bookmarks for important sites
- Real MetaMask: metamask.io (not .com)

**3. "Free" Giveaways**
- "Send 1 VPUSD, get 2 back" = SCAM
- No legitimate giveaway asks you to send first

**4. Too Good to Be True**
- 100% APY staking = SCAM
- "Guaranteed" profits = SCAM
- Unknown "investment opportunities" = SCAM

### If Something Goes Wrong

**Lost Access**:
- Use recovery phrase to restore wallet
- Install MetaMask on new device
- Select "Import using recovery phrase"
- Enter your 12 words
- Wallet restored!

**Sent to Wrong Address**:
- Unfortunately, transactions are irreversible
- Always double-check addresses
- Use address book for frequent recipients

**Suspected Fraud**:
- Contact VeritasPay support immediately
- Email: security@veritaspay.io
- We monitor for suspicious activity

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is VPUSD real money?**
A: Yes! Each VPUSD equals $1 USD. You can buy things, send to people, or convert to cash.

**Q: Is it like Bitcoin?**
A: No. Bitcoin's price changes wildly. VPUSD always equals $1, making it stable and reliable for payments.

**Q: Do I need to know about crypto?**
A: No! Just think of it as sending money on your phone. The "crypto" part happens behind the scenes.

**Q: Is it safe?**
A: Yes, when you follow security best practices. Never share your recovery phrase, and double-check addresses before sending.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: Transactions are irreversible. Always start with a small test amount when sending to a new address.

### Using VPUSD

**Q: Where can I spend VPUSD?**
A: Any merchant that accepts it, or convert to cash first. More merchants are accepting it daily.

**Q: Can I send to someone without a wallet?**
A: Yes! They can create a wallet in 5 minutes using the link/QR you send.

**Q: How long does a transaction take?**
A: Usually 10-30 seconds on Polygon network.

**Q: Can I cancel a transaction?**
A: No, once confirmed it's final. This prevents fraud but means you must be careful.

**Q: What's the minimum I can send?**
A: Technically $0.01, but gas fees make small amounts impractical. Send $10+ to be cost-effective.

### Technical

**Q: What's a gas fee?**
A: A small fee (usually $0.01) to process your transaction on the blockchain. Like a stamp for sending mail.

**Q: Why do I need MATIC for gas?**
A: MATIC is Polygon's native currency used to pay gas fees. You need a tiny amount (~$0.50 worth) to send transactions.

**Q: How do I get MATIC?**
A: Buy small amount on exchange, or use a faucet: https://faucet.polygon.technology

**Q: What if transaction fails?**
A: Rare, but can happen if gas too low. Try again with slightly higher gas.

### Money & Taxes

**Q: Do I pay taxes on VPUSD?**
A: Consult a tax professional. Generally:
- Using VPUSD = taxable event in many countries
- Report capital gains/losses
- Keep transaction records

**Q: Is this legal?**
A: Yes! VeritasPay operates in compliance with regulations in 100+ countries.

**Q: Is my money insured?**
A: VPUSD has an insurance pool for extreme events, but it's not FDIC insured like bank accounts. Only hold what you're comfortable with.

## Glossary

**Wallet**: Software that holds your VPUSD (like a bank account)
**Address**: Your wallet's "account number" (0x...)
**Recovery Phrase**: 12 words that restore your wallet
**Gas**: Small fee to process transactions
**MATIC**: Polygon's currency (used for gas)
**Network**: Blockchain where VPUSD exists (Polygon)
**Transaction**: Sending or receiving VPUSD
**QR Code**: Scannable code with your address
**Blockchain**: Digital ledger recording all transactions
